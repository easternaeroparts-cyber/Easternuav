      Unofficial planning aid. Udaan is built by Eastern UAV. It is not a CAAN product and it is not a permission to fly. Check current AIP Nepal, NOTAMs and CAAN before every flight.
      Rules: CAAN Unmanned Aircraft System Requirements (UASR) Issue 01, April 2021 · approval steps from the Nepal Drone Portal guidance. Airspace: AIP Nepal AD 1.3, AD 2 (runways, CTR/ATZ), ENR 2.1, 3.1, 3.3, 3.5, 5.1, 5.2, 5.5 (AMDT 10/2025) and the ENR 6.1 en-route chart (11 July 2025, georeferenced from its Lambert grid; runway positions for smaller aerodromes estimated from chart labels). Paragliding launch/landing sites from OpenStreetMap. Map data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) · geoJSON-Nepal (MIT) · OurAirports (public domain). Military sites and zone edges are approximate.
