Drone Pilot Courses

A staged pathway from your first controlled hover to running an approved operation — built on ICAO Annex 1 principles and mapped to CAAN requirements in Nepal and CASA Part 101 practice in Australia.

Nepal

CAAN pathway

Delivered from Kathmandu for Nepali operators, cooperatives, agencies and enterprise teams. Course material is written against Civil Aviation Authority of Nepal requirements, including the Personnel Licensing Requirements, and against ICAO Annex 1 principles.

Australia

CASA Part 101 pathway

Delivered from Brisbane for Australian operators and enterprise teams. Course material follows CASA Part 101 practice — remote pilot licensing, category endorsements and the operator certificate that commercial work sits under.

Eastern UAV is a new training operation building toward formal approval in both countries. Courses are structured to the syllabus requirements above, and where a course leads to a licence, endorsement or certificate, we confirm the issuing authority and the current approval position with you in writing before you enrol. We would rather tell you that plainly than have you find out later.

Foundation

Introduction to Drones Your first properly taught flights — controls, airmanship and the habits that keep an aircraft out of trouble. Drone Safety & Airspace Awareness Where you may fly, where you may not, and how to tell the difference before you take off — not after. Recreational to Commercial A bridging course for self-taught pilots: what changes the moment somebody pays you.

Licensing

Remote Pilot Licence The full theory and practical program to licence standard — the qualification commercial work is built on. Sub-25 kg Multirotor The standard commercial category — the class rating most professional drone work sits inside. Aeronautical Radio Operator Certificate Radio procedure and phraseology — required the moment your work takes you near controlled airspace. Licence Conversion For pilots already holding a remote pilot licence from another authority — we tell you what the real gap is.

Endorsements

Sub-150 kg Multirotor Heavy-lift operations — cargo, logistics, response and industrial platforms well beyond the standard category. VTOL & Powered Lift Hybrid aircraft that take off vertically and cruise on a wing — and the transition between the two. Agriculture & Spraying Spray and spreader operations — payload, calibration, chemical handling and the responsibility that comes with it. Night Operations Flying after last light — lighting requirements, orientation, and the visual illusions that catch people out.

Specialist

Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) Flying past where you can see the aircraft — and building the approval case that lets you. Mountain & High-Altitude Operations Density altitude, valley wind and terrain — flying where the aircraft has less to work with than the manual assumes. Emergency Services & Response Rapid tasking, scene mapping and working alongside responders when the situation is still moving. Thermal & Sensor Operations Flying the payload, not just the aircraft — thermal, multispectral and the data behind the picture.

Instructor & Organisational

Instructor & Assessor Program Train the trainer — instructional technique, competency assessment, and the records that stand up to audit. Operator Certificate Establishment Building the organisation behind the approval — manuals, procedures, safety management and the application itself. Chief Remote Pilot Preparation The accountable role — what it actually carries, and how to discharge it properly.

Groups & Institutions

Corporate & Agency Programs Training delivered on your sites, on your aircraft, against your operating procedures. School & University Programs Structured introductory delivery for students — aviation as a discipline, not a toy. Cooperative & NGO Intakes Group training for field teams — practical, applied, and built around the work you actually do. Recurrent & Currency Training Keeping a licensed team current — skills fade, procedures drift, and audits ask for evidence.
