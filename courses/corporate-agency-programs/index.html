Corporate & Agency Programs

Training delivered on your sites, on your aircraft, against your operating procedures.

Course Overview

Off-the-shelf training teaches generic operations. If your team flies the same three sites with the same two aircraft under a specific set of internal procedures, generic training leaves them to bridge the gap themselves — and they will bridge it inconsistently.

These programs are built around what your organisation actually does. We work from your sites, your platforms and your operating procedures, and where those procedures have gaps we tell you rather than teaching around them.

Delivered at your location in Nepal or Australia, for teams from a handful of pilots upward.

PrerequisitesVaries by program. We assess your team's starting point first.
FormatFully tailored. Scoped after a needs assessment — no fixed syllabus or duration.
LocationsKathmandu, Nepal · Brisbane, Australia · or at your site

Course Enquiry

We aim to respond within one business day.

What the course covers

  • Training needs assessment against your actual operations
  • Syllabus tailored to your platforms and sites
  • Delivery at your location, on your aircraft
  • Site-specific hazard and airspace briefing
  • Integration with your existing SOPs and safety system
  • Competency assessment and records handed back to you
  • Recurrent training schedule design
  • Review of your procedures, with honest feedback on gaps

Who it's for

  • Utilities, mining, telecom and infrastructure operators
  • Government departments and municipal agencies
  • Construction, surveying and engineering firms
