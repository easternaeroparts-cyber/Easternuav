Sub-150 kg Multirotor

Heavy-lift operations — cargo, logistics, response and industrial platforms well beyond the standard category.

Course Overview

Take your heavy-lift operations beyond the standard commercial category. Sub-150 kg multirotors carry real payloads — cargo, delivery loads, heavy sensor packages — and they are unforgiving in a way smaller aircraft are not.

A 25 kg aircraft that fails becomes a problem. A 100 kg aircraft that fails becomes an emergency. The margins, the site control, the exclusion zones and the pre-flight discipline all change, and this endorsement is where that is taught.

The course covers the platform you intend to operate, alongside a refresher on the fundamentals — mission planning, risk management and emergency procedures — applied at this weight class.

PrerequisitesRemote pilot licence and current multirotor experience. This is not an entry-level course.
FormatType-specific. Theory plus practical on the platform you will operate.
LocationsKathmandu, Nepal · Brisbane, Australia · or at your site

Course Enquiry

We aim to respond within one business day.

What the course covers

  • Regulatory position of the sub-150 kg category and what approval it requires
  • Systems, redundancy and failure modes on heavy-lift platforms
  • Load planning, centre of gravity and slung-load behaviour
  • Power budgeting and reserve margins at weight
  • Site control, exclusion zones and ground crew coordination
  • Launch and recovery site selection for a heavy aircraft
  • Emergency procedures where a controlled ditching is the best outcome
  • Type-specific training on your chosen aircraft
  • Practical assessment at category weight

Who it's for

  • Operators moving into cargo transport and logistics
  • Emergency services and disaster response teams
  • Industrial and infrastructure operators adopting heavy platforms
