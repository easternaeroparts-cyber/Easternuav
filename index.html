Training

The remote pilot pathway

A staged syllabus built on ICAO Annex 1 principles and mapped to Nepali (CAAN) and Australian (CASA Part 101) requirements — so a licence earned here means something to a regulator and to a client.

01

Entry-level program covering air law and airspace, meteorology, human factors, aircraft systems, battery and power management, pre-flight planning, emergency procedures and supervised practical flight to a defined competency standard.

Commercial operations

Missions we fly

Every job leaves a paper trail: flight plan, risk assessment, crew brief, log and deliverable. That is the difference between a drone operator and an aviation operator.

Survey & Mapping

Photogrammetry and orthomosaics, digital surface and terrain models, volumetric stockpile measurement, corridor mapping and progressive construction capture.

Asset & Infrastructure Inspection

Transmission lines and towers, penstocks and hydro assets, bridges, roofs and façades, telecom structures — close visual inspection without scaffolding or rope access.

Agriculture

Crop health and stress mapping, plantation and field inventory, irrigation assessment, and spray-operation planning for cooperatives and estates.

Broadcast, documentary, tourism and corporate work — flown by licensed crews with the permissions and insurance the production needs on file.

Aviation-Sector Work

Regulatory & advisory

Getting the framework right

Unmanned aviation only scales where the rules are clear and workable. We work both sides of that: helping operators meet the requirements, and helping authorities design requirements that can actually be met.

For operators

  • Operations manuals and SOPs
  • Certification and approval applications
  • Safety management system setup
  • Risk assessment and SORA-style analysis
  • Audit preparation and record systems

For authorities

  • RPAS regulatory framework development
  • Licensing and certification pathway design
  • Training-organisation approval criteria
  • Benchmarking against ICAO and CASA practice
  • Inspector and examiner familiarisation

For enterprise

  • In-house drone program design
  • Build-versus-contract assessment
  • Pilot competency and currency schemes
  • Data handling and privacy procedures
  • Insurance and liability structuring

About

Part of a working aviation group

Eastern UAV is the unmanned aviation arm of Eastern Aero, a diversified aviation group working across parts trading and MRO support, aircraft and engine leasing, asset management and aviation software, with offices in Kathmandu, Brisbane and the United States.

That heritage shapes how we run drone operations. Airline customers do not accept "it flew fine" as evidence — they want the document, the traceability and the procedure. We hold unmanned operations to the same line, and we teach our pilots to expect it.

Our focus is Nepal and Australia: two very different regulatory environments, one shared standard of professionalism.
